# DoorDispatcher — JUNIOR REP Training Video
### "Right doors. Less walking. More money."

**Audience:** BRAND-NEW junior reps (day one). On the map they knock **Inspection-needed** doors, re-work **Not-Interested** doors, sign the inspection, and handle go-backs.
**Film in practice mode:** `https://free-roof-inspections.netlify.app/?mode=harvest&test=jr&demo=1`
**Runtime:** ~8 min · **[SCREEN]** = what to record · **[SAY]** = paste into HeyGen · **[TEXT]** = on-screen caption

> **The vibe:** This is a motivation video disguised as a how-to. A new rep's #1 fear is "can I even do this?" — so every scene sells ONE idea: *this map hands you a veteran's brain on day one. You're not behind — you're ahead.* Sell the dream, paint the first win, make them want to run out the door. Avatar small in the corner; the screen and the words do the selling.
