## COLD OPEN — full-state → snap zoom ✅
- Pinch **all the way out** until you see the whole state peppered with dots.
- Then pinch/double-tap to **zoom hard into one neighborhood.** Record the zoom motion itself — that's the shot.

## SCENE 1 — The equalizer ✅
- Just sit on a neighborhood with **Inspection + Not-Interested** pins visible. Slowly pan. No taps — the loaded pins ARE the shot.

## SCENE 2 — Warm doors only (the lock) ✅
- Point the camera at the two pin colors.
- **Tap any pin (before you route anything)** → the card shows **"🔒 Homeowner & address show once this door is on your route."** That locked card is the shot.

## SCENE 3 — Route an area ✅
- Tap **▢ Route an area** (bottom-left, blue).
- **Drag a box** around a cluster of inspection pins → release.
- The route draws with **numbered stops + the line.** Zoom out slightly to show the whole loop.

## SCENE 4 — Work the door, zero paperwork ✅
- Tap **stop #1** → door card → tap **Not home.**
- Tap that **same pin again** → card → tap a different status (e.g. **Roof looks fine**) to show "re-status, nothing's locked." (Nothing saves — it's practice.)

## SCENE 5 — The money moment: sign it ⭐ ✅ *(one caution)*
- Tap a stop → card → **Sign Inspection** → the **Claim Intake opens pre-filled** with the address.
- Film through **confirm → submit**; the door flips **Sold.**
- ⚠️ **Caution:** the Sign/Intake flow can reach real systems even in demo. To be 100% safe, **use an obviously fake test address** (or stop the recording right at "Submit" and let me confirm demo blocks the write) so you don't create a real record.

## SCENE 6 — 🍀 Clover Leaf ⭐ ⚠️ *(needs an active install nearby)*
- Clover pins only exist around a **real roof install in progress** (a pulsing 🚧). Zoom to a neighborhood that currently has one and the 🚧 + green 🍀 pins load like any real pin.
- Tap the **🚧** (the install) → tap a **🍀 pin** → the card shows the **install homeowner's name** + the options.
- **Route the cluster** to show it lock — but the "🔒 another rep is working this" line needs a *second* rep on a live link, so film that half with a second phone on a real link, or drop a caption.
- **Inspection Needed top-up:** tap the **Inspection Needed** filter chip → more pins pour onto the map. ✅ (this part stages fine anywhere.)
- 👉 If no clover cluster is active when you film, tell me — I can add a **demo clover cluster** so you can stage it on demand.

## SCENE 7 — Hustle pays: self-gen ✅
- Tap the **drop-your-own-pin** control → tap an **un-pinned house.**
- The **"Owner occupied?"** county check runs (free lookup) → shows **Sign / Retail / Pending.** Works in demo.

## SCENE 8 — Never lose one: go-backs ⚠️ *(needs a rep with go-backs)*
- **Demo mode has no rep token, so go-backs won't load here.** Two ways to get the shot:
  1. Film from **your own real rep link** (a test rep that actually has go-backs) instead of the demo link, **or**
  2. Ask me to add a **"test go-backs" harness** to practice mode (like "Add test appt") so you can drop a few and film the list + the "Today's go-backs" card on demand.

## 🆕 ADD-IN SCENE — Review books itself ⭐ ⚠️ *(same as Scene 8)*
- Needs go-backs present, so use a **real rep link** or the **test-go-back harness** (see Scene 8).
- Once go-backs exist: the free "Route an area" button is replaced by the amber **"▶ Start my day · N required reviews"** — tap it → the tight review loop builds with fresh doors between. Open a review → **🚪 Nobody home — try again next time** → it re-dates.

## SCENE 9 — Accountability partner ✅
- After Scene 3's route, **work a few stops** (statuses updating), then pan along the route: **finished stops behind, the line ahead.** That live-updating route is the shot.

## SCENE 10 — How many rounds? ⭐ ✅
- Build a route, **work every stop** — mark a few **Not home** — reach the **last stop.**
- The app **rebuilds into Round 2** (the not-homes re-routed, visibly shorter). Work it down again → **Round 3**, shorter still. Record the shrink.

## SCENE 11 — The dare ✅
- After you've statused a bunch of doors in different colors this session, **zoom out slowly** on the worked neighborhood — pins in every color. (Statuses stay for the session; don't reload mid-shot.)
